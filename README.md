## The pitch

> We lease retired librarians to shush your noisy neighbours for a monthly fee, because light sleepers will pay anything for one quiet night.

One sentence, 139 characters. A bizarre startup idea that sounds like a joke
until the business logic lands: the customers are light sleepers in
apartments, the money is a monthly subscription, and anyone who has ever lain
awake listening to a neighbour's 2 a.m. karaoke knows exactly why they pay.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
when set, for local testing). No network, no secrets, no dependencies — just
the standard library.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
